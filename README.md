## 🌌・Overview
### (っ˘ω˘ς ) A Little Introduction ♡

Biological systems are wonderfully complex! (✧ω✧)

From microscopic structures to intricate physiological processes, living systems generate information that can be difficult to understand through conventional representations alone.

Three-dimensional visualization offers an opportunity to explore biological structures, relationships, and potentially physiological data in a more intuitive and engaging way.

BioSenses-3d explores this intersection of biology and computation, creating a foundation for future experimentation with biological visualization and interactive digital environments.

## 🔬・Scientific Considerations
### (｀・ω・´) Science First!

BioSenses-3d explores computational approaches to biological visualization. Any scientific or biomedical application should distinguish clearly between:

- 🎨 **Visualization** — A visual representation of biological structures or data.
- 🧮 **Simulation** — A computational model of a biological system or process.
- 🧪 **Experimental Validation** — Evidence that supports the accuracy or usefulness of a method or model.

These concepts are related, but they are not interchangeable! ( •̀ ω •́ )✧

Where biological measurements or physiological data are incorporated, their sources, units, processing methods, limitations, and validation procedures should be documented.

> ⚕️ **Medical Disclaimer:** This project should not be considered a validated medical device or diagnostic system unless the appropriate evidence and regulatory requirements have been established.
>
> *(｡•́︿•̀｡) Accuracy matters, especially when biology and human health are involved.*
